EX716 flight simulator
======================

Run from the tests directory:

    python3 ../cpu.py flightsim.asm

Use an 80-column terminal with mouse reporting. The simulation advances once
per second. Press q or Q to quit.

Controls
--------

Thrust bar (left, rows 15-21):
    Click lower for more thrust; click higher for less. The ### marker shows
    the setting. Thrust sets a target for speed rather than changing speed
    instantly.

Stick (center, rows 14-22):
    Click below the center to pull back and command nose-up pitch. Click above
    the center to push forward and command nose-down pitch. Horizontal stick
    position also commands rudder: left for left yaw, right for right yaw.
    The o marks the last stick position.

Aileron (right, horizontal track):
    Click left or right of the center mark to roll the aircraft. Moving the
    control back to center stops the roll; it does not level an existing bank.
    Apply opposite aileron to level the wings.

Flaps (vertical guide):
    Click lower for more flap, higher for less. Flaps add lift and drag in
    the current simplified model.

Keyboard controls
-----------------

Each keypress moves its control one notch. The mouse controls remain active,
and both input methods update the same on-screen indicators.

    4 / 6       Aileron left / right (^ on the horizontal track)
    8 / 2       Pitch nose down / up (o on the stick)
    5           Center aileron and rudder; pitch stays where it is
    0 / .       Rudder left / right (^ on the rudder track and o on the stick)
    + / -       More / less thrust (### on the thrust bar)
    [ / ]       Raise / lower flaps (< on the flap guide)

These are printable keys. A numeric keypad may send different escape
sequences depending on the terminal; the ordinary number keys are supported.

The upper panel shows altitude, compass heading, speed, roll, bank, pitch,
turn rate, vertical velocity, engine, and fuel. X and Y at the lower left
show the aircraft's current map position. Heading 0 is north, 90 east,
180 south, and 270 west.

S is the starting position (0, 0), facing north. A-E are the current
letter markers for planned airport locations. They are ground markers only:
there are no runway surfaces or landing rules yet. The ^ symbols form one
connected mountain ridge, drawn as a coarse line silhouette in the forward
viewport. The ridge is not solid terrain and has no collision detection.

Coordinates and flight units are still provisional. The proposed scale of
1/1,000 nautical mile per X/Y unit, speed in knots, and an altitude ceiling
of 10,000 feet have not yet been applied to the flight math. The listed
ridge elevations are stored as feet, but the projection is an intentionally
coarse integer approximation for the text viewport.
